## Overview
A Streamlit app that reads handwritten answers with OCR and scores them against reference answers using LLM.

## Results
- Tested on 50+ handwritten sheets, 5 to 10 questions each.
- About 80 to 90% accuracy on clearly written answers.
- About 30 seconds per sheet, against 2 to 3 minutes by hand.
- Generates scores and a CSV report automatically.
